# Community participation

Be respectful, constructive and considerate of other contributors. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and unsolicited commercial solicitation are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private information.

Maintainers may moderate or remove content and restrict participation to protect the community. To raise a concern privately, use the contact route at https://a2zsoc.com and request a private discussion. This repository is independently maintained; CNCF's committees are not its default moderators.
